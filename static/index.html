Official Virginia DMV Exam Format & Rules

The Virginia knowledge exam consists of 40 total questions divided into two strictly enforced sections:

Section 1: Traffic Signs (10 Questions)

Tests sign shapes, colors, and meanings. Passing Requirement: 100% (10 out of 10). If you miss even one sign, the computerized kiosk immediately discontinues the test.

Section 2: General Knowledge (30 Questions)

Covers traffic laws, following distances, speed limits, and DUI rules. Passing Requirement: 80% (24 out of 30). The test ends once you reach 24 correct or 7 incorrect.
